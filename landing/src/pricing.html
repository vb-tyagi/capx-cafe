Before you choose

A few
good questions.

Can I start without joining the creator waitlist?

Yes. The developer option uses your own X app. You need a compatible coding agent, Node ≥ 22.6, an allowed email, and the server URL. Ask about hosted access or self-host.

Is self-hosting another paid plan?

No. It is a deployment choice for the open-source server. You pay for your infrastructure, your X API usage, and your agent as applicable. You also become the operator responsible for keys, updates, and the service.

What happens when an allowance runs out?

A send needing that category cannot proceed until allowance is available. For the planned creator beta, eligible packs can add capacity for the current cycle. A pack never removes the Short plan’s restrictions on links or threads.

How do loops and daily limits work?

A loop publishes prepared posts on a recurring server-side schedule. Loops require a verified X account at least 30 days old and must meet account eligibility checks. Safety caps of 10 posts/hour and 40/day per account apply across both lanes, even when monthly allowance remains. These are ceilings, not recommended posting targets.

What are the thread and reply limits?

A thread contains at most 10 posts. Thread posts cannot contain URLs. Quote reposts are not supported, and replies can only chain onto your own posts. Each post in a thread consumes a total-post allowance on creator plans.

Does a creator plan include AI writing?

Your coding agent writes the content using the context you provide. capx café provides publishing tools and guardrails. An agent subscription or model usage is separate from the planned creator prices.

What does the guardrail cover?

It checks post text and publishing conditions. It does not inspect attached images or videos, and a passing verdict is not a guarantee of accuracy or good judgment. See built-in safety and the full security model.
